## Reference

### Effort

- Recovery: RPE 2–3
- Easy / LDR: RPE 3–4
- Tempo / Threshold: RPE 6–7
- MP: controlled marathon effort; able to keep going at the finish
- Strides: 4–6 × 20 seconds, fast but relaxed, fully recover between reps

### Strength A

2–3 sets, always leave 2–3 reps in reserve.

- Bulgarian split squat · 8–10 / side
- Single-leg RDL · 8–10 / side
- Glute bridge or hip thrust · 10–15
- Single-leg calf raise · 12–15 / side
- Lateral band walk · 10–15 / side
- Plank or dead bug · 30–45 seconds

### Foot and Ankle Routine

- Short foot / arch activation
- Single-leg balance
- Straight-knee and bent-knee calf raises
- Toe yoga
- Light glute medius work

### LDR Checklist

- Normal familiar breakfast; do not start 20–30 km fasted
- First 5 km should feel too slow
- Fuel every 30–40 minutes and drink water
- Check left arch → left calf → right quad
- Stop if pain changes form or keeps escalating

### Pain Signal

- Green · 0–2/10: continue
- Yellow · 3–4/10: reduce or replace the next quality session
- Red · 5+/10: stop; the plan never overrides pain

Easy really easy. One quality run is enough. LDR is the priority. Arrive healthy on Oct 25.
